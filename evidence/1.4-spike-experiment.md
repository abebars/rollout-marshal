## What the sample shows

- The warehouse connection (id 11143) reports `status: pending_connection` but `total_events_received: 300`,
  `unique_events_count: 2` (`spike_event`, `$flag_exposure`) and `last_event_received_at` 22:24:35.536Z,
  under a second after invocation 2's flush (B's evidence, 22:24:35.962Z end).
- Exposures: `cheaper` 78, `control` 72, matching B's router invocation.
- Results for metric 858 (`spike_metric`): `cheaper` n 78, sum 63 (mean 0.8077); `control` n 72, sum 14
  (mean 0.1944). Both equal what B's judge invocation sent, so outcomes from a second client join the
  exposures per identity.
- `inference.cheaper`: `lift` 3.1538 = (0.8077 - 0.1944) / 0.1944, so lift is RELATIVE and a FRACTION;
  `ci_low` 1.1358 and `ci_high` 5.1719 bound it (design.md section 7 calls the lower bound
  `helpful_lift_ci_lower`; the level is not in the response, the docs say 95 percent); `chance_to_win`
  0.9989 is the win probability, which never goes in `prob_cheaper_not_worse`.
- There is no 'not worse' probability, so `prob_cheaper_not_worse` stays null and the promote test is
  `ci_low` >= -0.05 with both guardrails (design.md section 6). The results label the control arm
  `control`; 2.2 maps it to `current`. Per-variant values are `n`, `sum` and `sum_squares`; the mean is
  sum / n. `is_final` is false while the experiment runs; `srm_p_value` is null.
