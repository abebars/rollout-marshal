- **What it shows.** `spike_kill_switch` (feature `306318`, Development `119136`) went off
  and back on, each change through the REST form of 1.3's four steps:
  1. `POST .../versions/` creates a version holding a copy of the live states (201).
  2. `GET .../versions/<uuid>/featurestates/` lists them; the environment default is the
     one with `feature_segment` null.
  3. `PUT .../featurestates/<id>/` with `{"feature", "enabled", "feature_state_value"}`
     updates it (200).
  4. `POST .../versions/<uuid>/publish/` with `{}` makes it live (200, `live_from` in the
     reply).
- **The read-backs.** The live state, read back through the features list with
  `environment=119136`, was `enabled False`, then `enabled True`. The feature ends as it
  started.
- **A finding.** The publish reply carries `live_from` and no `uuid`, hence `published
  version None` above. `set_state()` now returns the version's UUID from its create step.
  `app/tests/flagsmith_fake.py` answers the publish as Flagsmith did.
- **The test.** `test_set_state_takes_the_four_v2_steps_and_changes_nothing_until_published`
  pins the four requests (method, path and body), and `test_v2_probe_turns_the_feature_off_then_on_and_reads_each_back` pins the probe.
- **Paths and bodies.** They come from Flagsmith's published OpenAPI spec,
  https://api.flagsmith.com/api/v1/swagger.json, downloaded at 04:04:55Z:
  - `create_environment_feature_version` is `POST`;
  - `get_environment_feature_version_states` is `GET`;
  - `update_environment_feature_version_state` is `PUT`, and
    `CustomEnvironmentFeatureVersionFeatureState` requires `feature_state_value`;
  - `publish_environment_feature_version` is `POST`.
