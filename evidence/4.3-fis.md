## Run half

To be filled after 3.6 and the recalibrated threshold. The lead applies `infra/modules/fis` first.

- Seed: `seed_experiment.py rfault<HHMM>`
- Load: `load.py --run-id <run_id> --users 150 --questions 2`
- Fault: `scripts/inject_fault.sh`, giving the experiment ID and its start and end times
- The alarm: the time to ALARM after the fault starts (within 5 minutes), and the time it leaves ALARM after the fault ends
- **Human** ends the experiment, then `reset.py`
