## The split, SRM and the option's allocation: as expected

- **The 65/85 split** is an ordinary draw for 150 identities under 50/50. The exact
  two-sided binomial p is 0.12 (row 28), and task 1.4's experiment 807 split 78 to 72.
- **The null `srm_p_value`** is expected. The Statistics Glossary
  (docs.flagsmith.com/experimentation/statistics) does two things:
  - it lists the SRM check as "Planned";
  - it says the check "only checks once there are at least 100 identities", and warns at a
    one-in-a-thousand imbalance. That is far from p = 0.12.
- **The option's `default_percentage_allocation` of 0.0** (row 26, while 1127 ran) is
  expected:
  - `seed_experiment.py` sends 50 when it creates the option;
  - design.md section 4's Treatment row says the experiment's rollout block holds the 50
    percent "and it decides the split whatever the option's own default allocation";
  - task 1.4's options 37532 and 37546 read 0.0 the same way
    (`evidence/1.4-spike-experiment.md`).

  Here the block said 50, and cheaper was served to 85 identities.
- **Observed, not explained:** `is_final` stayed false 4 minutes 38 seconds after
  `ended_at`. docs.flagsmith.com/experimentation/analyse-an-experiment says ending an
  experiment freezes its results "as final". No refresh was asked for after the end, and I
  have not looked further.
